How to measure your cycle length

Your cycle length is the number of days from the first day of one period to the first day of the next. Count the start day of the new period, not the end of the last one. Averaging several cycles gives a more useful number than a single cycle, because individual cycles often vary by a few days.

What is a normal cycle length?

For adults, cycles between 21 and 35 days are commonly seen. Teenagers and people approaching menopause often have longer or more variable cycles.

Why does my cycle length change?

Stress, illness, travel, changes in weight or exercise, some medicines and hormonal changes can all shift a cycle by several days.
