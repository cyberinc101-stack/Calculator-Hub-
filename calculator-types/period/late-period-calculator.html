What counts as a late period?

A period is late when it has not started by the date you expected it. Add your usual cycle length to the first day of your last period to get that date, then count the days since. A delay of a few days is common and often has an ordinary cause such as stress, illness or travel.

When should I take a pregnancy test?

Most home tests can be used from the first day of a missed period. If the result is negative and your period still has not arrived, testing again a few days later is reasonable.

When should I see a doctor?

If your period is more than six weeks late, if you have missed several periods, or if you have severe pain, very heavy bleeding or other symptoms that worry you.
