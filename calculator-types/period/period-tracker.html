    How the period tracker works

    Enter the first day of each period. The tracker measures the gap between each start date, averages your recent cycles, and adds that average to your last start date to predict the next one. Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before the next period, and the fertile window is the five days before ovulation plus ovulation day and the day after.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Dates are stored in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to our servers, and they are removed if you clear your browser data. They will not appear on other devices.

    How accurate are the predictions?

    They are estimates. Stress, illness, travel, sleep and hormonal changes can all move a period by several days. The more cycles you log, the closer the average gets to your usual pattern.
