How to calculate your next period

Count from the first day of your last period. Add your cycle length to that date to get the first day of your next period. The cycle length is the number of days from the first day of one period to the first day of the next, and it is usually between 21 and 35 days for adults.

To find your fertile window, count back 14 days from your next period to estimate ovulation. Sperm can survive for up to five days, so the window runs from five days before ovulation to the day after.

What if my cycle length changes each month?

Use your average, or work it out with the cycle length calculator. If your cycles vary a lot, the dates here will be less reliable.

Can I use this to avoid pregnancy?

No. Ovulation timing varies from cycle to cycle, so this calculator is not a method of contraception.
