About Milligrams and Grams

The milligram is a tiny metric unit of mass equal to one-thousandth of a gram, commonly used in medicine dosages and chemistry.

The gram is a small metric unit of mass equal to one-thousandth of a kilogram, commonly used for cooking, jewelry, and precise measurements.
