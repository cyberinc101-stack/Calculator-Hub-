Multiply the carats value by 0.2 to get grams.

About Carats and Grams

The carat is a unit of mass used to weigh gemstones and pearls, equal to 200 milligrams.

The gram is a small metric unit of mass equal to one-thousandth of a kilogram, commonly used for cooking, jewelry, and precise measurements.
