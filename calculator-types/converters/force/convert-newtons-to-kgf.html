How to Convert Newtons to Kilogram-Force

Multiply the newtons value by 0.101972 to get kilogram-force.

About Newtons and Kilogram-Force

The newton is the SI unit of force, defined as the force needed to accelerate one kilogram at one meter per second squared.

Kilogram-force is a unit of force based on the weight of one kilogram under standard gravity, sometimes used in older engineering contexts.
