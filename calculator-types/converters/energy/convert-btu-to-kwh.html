How to Convert BTU to Kilowatt-Hours

Multiply the btu value by 0.000293071 to get kilowatt-hours.

About BTU and Kilowatt-Hours

The British Thermal Unit (BTU) is a unit of energy used to describe heating and cooling capacity, such as for air conditioners and furnaces.

The kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy used to measure electricity consumption, commonly seen on home power bills.
