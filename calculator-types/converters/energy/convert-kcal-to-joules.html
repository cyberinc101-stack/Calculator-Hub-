How to Convert Kilocalories to Joules

Multiply the kilocalories value by 4184 to get joules.

About Kilocalories and Joules

The kilocalorie (often just called 'calorie' on food labels) is a unit of energy equal to 1,000 calories.

The joule is the standard scientific unit of energy, used in physics to measure work, heat, and electrical energy.
