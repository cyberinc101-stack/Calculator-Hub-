How to Convert Watts to Kilowatts

Multiply the watts value by 0.001 to get kilowatts.

About Watts and Kilowatts

The watt is the standard unit of power, measuring the rate of energy use, commonly seen on appliance and light bulb labels.

The kilowatt is a unit of power equal to 1,000 watts, commonly used to describe appliance power and electric vehicle motor output.
