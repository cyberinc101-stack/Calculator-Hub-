About Megawatts and Kilowatts

The megawatt is a unit of power equal to 1,000 kilowatts, used to describe large-scale power generation such as power plants.

The kilowatt is a unit of power equal to 1,000 watts, commonly used to describe appliance power and electric vehicle motor output.
