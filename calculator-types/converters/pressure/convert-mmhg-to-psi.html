About mmHg and PSI

Millimeters of mercury (mmHg) is a unit of pressure traditionally used to measure blood pressure and atmospheric pressure.

Pounds per square inch (PSI) is a unit of pressure commonly used in the US for tire pressure, water pressure, and mechanical systems.
