About Bar and Atmospheres

The bar is a metric unit of pressure roughly equal to atmospheric pressure at sea level, commonly used in weather forecasting and engineering.

The atmosphere (atm) is a unit of pressure based on average air pressure at sea level, commonly used in chemistry and meteorology.
