About Teaspoons and Milliliters

The teaspoon is a small cooking measurement of volume, commonly used for spices, baking powder, and small ingredient quantities.

The milliliter is a small metric unit of volume equal to one-thousandth of a liter, commonly used for medicine doses and small liquid measurements.
