How to Convert Square Centimeters to Square Inches

Multiply the square centimeters value by 0.155 to get square inches.

About Square Centimeters and Square Inches

The square centimeter is a small metric unit of area used for measuring small surfaces in science and engineering.

The square inch is a small imperial unit of area used for measuring small surfaces like screens or materials.
