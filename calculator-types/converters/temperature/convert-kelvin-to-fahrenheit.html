How to Convert Kelvin to Fahrenheit

Multiply the kelvin value by 1.8, then add -459.67, to get fahrenheit.

About Kelvin and Fahrenheit

Kelvin is the scientific base unit of temperature, starting at absolute zero, used in physics, chemistry, and engineering.

Fahrenheit is the temperature scale used primarily in the United States, where water freezes at 32° and boils at 212°.
