About Miles and Nautical Miles

The mile is an imperial unit of distance equal to 5,280 feet, used primarily in the United States and the United Kingdom for road distances and speed limits.

The nautical mile is a unit of distance used in air and sea navigation, equal to 1,852 meters and based on one minute of latitude.
