How to Convert Meters to Fathoms

Multiply the meters value by 0.546807 to get fathoms.

About Meters and Fathoms

The meter is the base unit of length in the metric system, used worldwide for everyday length measurements from room sizes to running distances.

The fathom is a unit of length traditionally used to measure water depth, equal to 6 feet.
