About Light-Years and Kilometers

A light-year is the distance light travels in one year, used in astronomy to measure vast distances between stars and galaxies.

The kilometer is the standard metric unit for measuring medium to long distances, equal to 1,000 meters. It's the primary distance unit used on road signs and for trip distances across most of the world.
