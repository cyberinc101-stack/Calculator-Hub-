About Millimeters and Micrometers

The millimeter is a small metric unit of length equal to one-thousandth of a meter, used for precise measurements like screen thickness or engineering tolerances.

The micrometer (micron) is a unit of length equal to one-millionth of a meter, used to measure tiny distances like microchip features.
