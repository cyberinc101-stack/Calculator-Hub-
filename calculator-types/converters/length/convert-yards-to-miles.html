How to Convert Yards to Miles

Multiply the yards value by 0.000568182 to get miles.

About Yards and Miles

The yard is an imperial unit of length equal to 3 feet, traditionally used in sports fields and fabric measurements.

The mile is an imperial unit of distance equal to 5,280 feet, used primarily in the United States and the United Kingdom for road distances and speed limits.
