How to Convert Gradians to Degrees

About Gradians and Degrees

The gradian (or gon) is a unit of angle measurement where a full circle equals 400 gradians, occasionally used in surveying.

The degree is the common unit for measuring angles, with a full circle equal to 360 degrees.
