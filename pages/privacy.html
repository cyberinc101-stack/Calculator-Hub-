Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

CalcHub provides free calculator tools. All calculations run entirely in your browser — no inputs or results are sent to our servers.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for or store personal information, and we do not store anything you enter into our calculators. No account is required. Our analytics and advertising partners collect some data automatically, as described below.

3. Analytics

CalcHub uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which calculators are most popular. It collects usage information such as the pages you visit, your approximate location, and your device and browser type, and it uses cookies or similar identifiers to do so. We only look at aggregated reports and do not attempt to identify individual visitors. Google's use of analytics data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

CalcHub is partnered with Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve personalised ads based on your browsing history. Google's collection and use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads or visit aboutads.info/choices.

5. Cookies

CalcHub stores your light/dark mode preference in your browser's localStorage. No tracking cookies are set by CalcHub itself. Google AdSense and Google Analytics may set their own cookies as described in sections 3 and 4 above.

6. Third-Party Links

CalcHub may link to external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

7. Your Choices

You can opt out of personalised Google ads at google.com/settings/ads. You can also manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via our Contact page.
